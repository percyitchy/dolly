Dolly

Six ways to cut it.

Same recording, same features, same words. Only the shape of the editor changes. Open one, then tick Preview as Pro or press Show upgrade sheet at the top.

1Rails

Stage, tool rail, full timeline. The editor people already know.

Best for
Anyone arriving from another editor
Risk
Looks like every other screen recorder
Upgrade
Centred modal

2Shot List

Every zoom is a card with a thumbnail. You edit a script, and the timeline only places it.

Best for
Fast review of what the camera will do
Risk
Long recordings make a long list
Upgrade
Bottom sheet

3Monitor

The picture fills the window. Controls float over it and step aside.

Best for
Judging the video at full size
Risk
Panels cover the frame while you edit
Upgrade
Small popover beside the click

4Camera Track

The timeline is the hero. Zooms are drawn as the camera's real move, with ramps you can grab.

Best for
People who want precise control
Risk
Smallest preview, most to take in
Upgrade
Replaces the export column in place

5Three-Up

16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 side by side. Tick the shapes you want and export them together.

Best for
Posting one video to several places
Risk
Batch export adds build scope
Upgrade
Right-hand drawer

6One Take

The video is done when you stop recording. Change a little, or just export.

Best for
First-time users and quick demos
Risk
Zoom blocks are small until you open the full timeline
Upgrade
Sheet dropping from the title bar
