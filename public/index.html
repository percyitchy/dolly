What it does

Every recording comes out looking edited.

You press record and use your app. Dolly watches the clicks and the cursor, then builds the camera moves for you. You can drag any zoom on the timeline if you want a different shot.

2.0× · per click

Auto-zoom

Each click becomes a zoom on the timeline. Clicks close together share one shot, so the camera doesn't bounce.

spring, not linear

Smooth cursor

Shaky mouse paths get replaced with a clean glide between the points you actually clicked.

8 wallpapers

Backgrounds and padding

Your screen sits on a wallpaper with rounded corners and a soft shadow. Add your own image too.

circle · rounded

Webcam bubble

Record your face at the same time and park it in any corner. It shrinks out of the way when the camera zooms.

on-device

Captions

Speech turns into captions on your own machine with Whisper. Nothing is sent to a server.

Questions

Before you hit record.

Is it really free?

Yes. Recording, auto-zoom, smooth cursor and 1080p export are free forever, and the code is MIT licensed. Pro is a one-time $9.99 for 4K, 60 fps, captions, webcam, GIF export and the vertical cut.

How do I activate Pro?

After you pay you get a license key on Whop. Paste it in Settings once, and Pro keeps working offline. One key works on up to 3 devices.

Which permissions does it need?

On a Mac you allow Screen Recording, Input Monitoring and the microphone once in System Settings. macOS's wording mentions keystrokes, but Dolly only asks for mouse events, so it never sees what you type.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

The build isn't signed by Apple yet. The README shows the one extra step to open it. Until it's signed, macOS may ask for permissions again after an update.

Can I edit the zooms?

Every zoom is a block on the timeline. Drag it, stretch it, change the zoom level or delete it.

How was it built?

With Claude Code on Opus 5.5. The full source is on GitHub.
